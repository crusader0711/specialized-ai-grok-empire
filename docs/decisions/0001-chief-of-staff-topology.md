# ADR-0001: Single Chief-of-Staff topology
**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** Uncoordinated multi-bot teams create chat clutter, routing ambiguity, and runaway delegation.
**Decision:** One Chief of Staff is the front door for high-level topics; specialists never delegate; depth limit 2; reports bypass the CoS.
**Consequences:** Simple audit trail; the CoS can become a bottleneck, so reports go direct.
